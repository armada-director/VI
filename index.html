Event Announcement

ARTEMIS ARMADA VI

The Cosmos Chronicles

Capital University · Columbus, Ohio
July 9 – 11, 2027

Join us for the next chapter of Artemis Armada! Artemis Armada VI: The Cosmos Chronicles will deliver a more immersive experience than ever before, with dedicated spaces for each bridge and small craft, private communications, and an epic campaign chronicling the Terran Stellar Diaspora.

Event Highlights

Artemis Armada

  • Ticket Price: Capped at $100 per person — includes lodging
  • Capacity: Space for 10 bridges plus 10 small craft (fighters)
  • Equipment: Capital University and the Armada event team will provide all machines — attendees need bring nothing
  • Immersive Experience: A stronger focus on immersion than previous Armadas
  • Each bridge will be in its own dedicated space, separated by walls, partitions, or buildings
  • Small craft pilots will have their own separate space
  • Ship-to-ship and ship-to-command communications via a private TeamSpeak 3 / Mumble server

Crew Assignment

  • Attendees who want to be on the same crew will be placed together
  • Attendees without a preference will be assigned to fill gaps or form new bridges as needed

Lodging

  • Lodging at Capital University is included in the ticket price
  • Additional lodging options are available nearby (not covered by the ticket)

Food

  • Attendees are responsible for their own food costs
  • Several economical on-campus dining options are available
  • A wide variety of restaurants are located close to campus
  • Important: No food or drink is allowed on the bridges

Transportation

  • By car: Plenty of free parking available on campus
  • By air: Flying into Port Columbus Airport (CMH)? We will coordinate a complimentary shuttle to pick you up

The Cosmos Chronicles

Bridge crews will take part in scenarios that chronicle the Terran Stellar Diaspora. Crews will receive ratings based on their performance in each scenario. Most scenarios will be set in the frontier region known as the “Eastern Front” and will be organized into Acts.

Note: The following outline is one possible structure and is subject to change.

Act One: Expansion

  • Most advanced ship available at the start: Battleship
  • Only race encountered at the beginning: Kraliens
  • First-contact opportunities

Act Two: A House Divided

  • Civil war has broken out among some of the colonies
  • Other races may become involved on either side
  • Potential player-versus-player (PVP) action

Act Three: Galactic Conflict

  • Primarily large-scale fleet actions

Optional Family Programming

If there is sufficient interest, bridge crews may bring “civilians” (non-Artemis players such as spouses and children). Special programming will be available, including:

  • Visits to the Columbus Zoo and COSI (Center of Science and Industry)
  • Possible family-oriented events hosted by Capital University
  • Any off-campus family events will carry a separate price
  • All children must have a responsible adult with them at all times – no babysitting services offered

We look forward to seeing you among the stars!
